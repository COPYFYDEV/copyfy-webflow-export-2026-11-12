FAQ about Shopify for 1$

How do I lower my Shopify fees after the promo?

Once the Shopify promotional period wraps up you have several avenues to curtail your expenses. You can switch to a cheaper plan or opt for an annual subscription to potentially save up to 25%. Leveraging Shopify Payments can also trim transaction costs.

Feel free to engage in talks with payment processors; streamline running costs; or reach out to Shopify support for tailored guidance. As a last resort you can downgrade your plan but bear in mind that the "Starter" plan is earmarked for first-time users alone.

Is there a free alternative to Shopify for long-term use?

Indeed, there are complimentary substitutes to Shopify for sustained use. WooCommerce and Magento Open Source stand out as two favored picks. WooCommerce is a no-cost open-source WordPress extension enabling you to fashion an online storefront.

Magento Open Source represents another no-charge platform ideally suited for enterprises demanding extensive customization and expandability. Both entail overseeing your own web hosting domain name and SSL certificate.

What happens if I cancel during the 3-month Shopify promo?

If you opt to terminate your Shopify account during the trial run without picking a paid subscription no intervention is needed, and your account goes into hibernation. Should you select a pricing tier before your trial period concludes you'll need to manually put your store on hold or deactivate it to cancel your account.

You'll find an option to put your subscription on hold. The Pause and Build mode works wonders when you're keen on tweaking your store — be it adding fresh inventory. Under this arrangement your clientele can browse your store but purchases are disabled; expect a reduced subscription charge of $9 during this interval.

Are all Shopify plans eligible for the $1 for 3 months deal?

Regrettably not every Shopify plan qualifies for the $1 for 3 months deal. The special offer is applicable to specific plans as emphasized by the phrase "on select plans" on Shopify's advertising pages. The Basic Shopify and Advanced plans are up for grabs at $1 each month for the initial three months.

This incentive is crafted to draw budding entrepreneurs into launching their web-based shop by presenting a budget-friendly introductory phase. The promotional deal isn't extended to the Starter Retail and Plus plans.
