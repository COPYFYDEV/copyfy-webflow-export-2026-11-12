Why use an adspy tool for your store?

Before paying for a new creative, look at what brands in your market are advertising. An adspy tool connects the product, offer and advertising message. Build a list of ideas to test from concrete examples, while remembering that a visible ad is not proof of a profitable campaign.

Study your competitors’ ads
Search for a brand or keyword to see featured products, promotions and selling points. Compare several ads before choosing an angle.
Find angles for your next tests
Break down the opening hook, product demonstration and offer. Use the problem being solved as inspiration for an ad that fits your audience and brand.
Choose which creatives to study
Compare running time and engagement with the available data. An ad that has been active for a long time deserves a closer look, but does not prove profitability.

Can I save or download the ads I find?

Yes. Save useful ads and download available creatives to organize your research. Use them as references for your own ads while respecting the rights of brands and creators.

Can I search for ads by brand or keyword?

Yes. Use brands, keywords and filters to focus your search. Then compare ads for the same product or type of offer.

Does adspy data prove that an ad is profitable?

No. Running time, engagement, reach and spend are research signals, not proof of profit. Copyfy provides Meta reach and spend data for ads shown in the European Union. They do not reveal a competitor’s ROAS or profit margin.

How can I use adspy to find advertising ideas?

Start with a brand, product or keyword in your niche. Narrow your search with filters, compare several ads and note their hooks, demonstrations and offers. Turn those observations into ideas to test with your own creatives.

What is an adspy tool?

An adspy tool helps you find and analyze ads run by other brands. Compare creatives, offers and available signals to plan your own advertising tests. Copyfy is an ad research tool; this page does not describe the AdSpy.com service.
