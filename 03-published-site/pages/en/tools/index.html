Outils gratuits

CPA calculator
Enter your ad spend, your purchases and the costs of an order. The calculator compares your CPA to your break-even CPA, works out your profit per order and tells you which CPC or conversion rate to aim for to stay profitable. Free, no account needed.
Customer review analyzer
Paste an AliExpress, Amazon or Shopify product link. The tool reads the public reviews and builds a picture of the buyer: what makes them buy, what holds them back, who they buy for and the words they use. Free, no account needed.
Facebook ads downloader
Paste the link of an ad from the Meta Ad Library and download its videos as HD MP4s, its images and its copy, carousels and dynamic creatives included. Free, no account and no extension.
TikTok ads downloader
Paste the link of a TikTok Creative Center ad and get the video as an MP4, from 360p to 1080p, with its ad copy, campaign objective and target countries. Free, no account, no extension.
Ecommerce ROI calculator
Work out what your store really returns on the money you put in: ROI, gross and net profit, ROAS, profit margin and break-even point. Calculate a whole period or a single product, in your browser, with no account.
Customer lifetime value (CLV) calculator - free and instant
Work out what a customer is really worth over the whole relationship, not just on the first order. Enter your average order value, purchase frequency, margin and retention, and see the highest acquisition cost your store can afford.
Shopify product exporter
Pull the entire product catalogue of any Shopify store: titles, descriptions, variants, prices, image URLs and SEO metas. The file comes out in the Shopify import format, so it drops straight into another store. Free, no account needed.
Shopify subscription calculator
Compare the real cost of the Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus plans. Enter your revenue and your orders, see which one costs you least and at what volume you should switch.
Shopify theme detector
Find out which Shopify theme any store runs. Paste a URL and get the theme name, its maker, its price and a direct link to the Theme Store.
Shopify apps detector
See which apps any Shopify store uses. Paste a URL and get the full app list in seconds — free, no sign-up.
Dropshipping profit margin calculator
Calculate your net margin in 30 seconds and find out what you really keep from every sale after product, shipping, transaction and advertising costs, so you can finally stay profitable in dropshipping.
BEROAS calculator
Calculate your advertising break-even point in 30 seconds and discover the minimum ROAS to reach for your campaigns to be truly profitable. No complicated formulas: enter your numbers, we'll do the math for you!
